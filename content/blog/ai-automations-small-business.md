Every small business has a handful of repetitive tasks eating ten or more hours a week. The same intake forms, the same FAQ replies, the same follow-ups. Every one of them can be automated with AI — often in a weekend with no-code tools.

Here are seven automations that pay for themselves, ranked by how quickly they produce visible results.

## 1. AI chat support for your most-asked questions

Your customers ask the same ten questions — hours, pricing, turnaround. An AI assistant trained on your pages answers them instantly, 24/7, and hands off to a human when it should.

**Where to start:** connect a chatbot to your website content. Test with the FAQ and service pages first, then measure how many conversations it resolves without a human.

## 2. Lead capture that routes itself

Instead of leads sitting in an inbox, an automation can grade each enquiry, send an instant acknowledgement, add the contact to your CRM, and notify the right person.

**The setup:** a web form → an automation platform → your CRM or spreadsheet. Name, source and interest are captured automatically on every submission.

## 3. Auto follow-up and re-engagement

Most sales happen on the reminder you never send. An AI workflow can send a polite follow-up 24 hours, 3 days and 7 days after first contact, varying the message based on how the lead responded.

**Result:** fewer dropped leads, more appointments booked, with zero manual chasing.

## 4. Content repurposing

One recorded video becomes a blog post, three social captions, a newsletter section and a transcript — automatically. AI rewrites the same source material for each channel's tone.

**Where to start:** record one voice-over, then run it through a repurposing workflow. A library of content grows from a single hour of input.

## 5. Meeting notes and action items

Give an AI assistant your meeting recording and it returns a clean summary, decisions made, and assigned action items with owners and deadlines.

**Why it matters:** no more digging through a 45-minute recording for the one decision everyone forgot.

## 6. Automated invoicing and payment chasing

When a project completes, an AI workflow generates the invoice, sends it, tracks payment status, and sends a friendly reminder if it's overdue.

**The win:** cash flow stays predictable without the awkward "just checking in" emails.

## 7. Reporting that writes itself

Pull sales, support and marketing numbers from your tools and have AI write a one-page weekly summary with the metric that matters most identified.

**The payoff:** you spend Mondays deciding, not compiling.

## How to pick your first automation

Resist the urge to automate everything at once. Use this filter:

1. **Frequency** — does it happen more than once a week?
2. **Time** — does it take longer than 15 minutes each time?
3. **Rules** — is the decision mostly repeatable?
4. **Payoff** — is a mistake cheap and a hit valuable?
